# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone
who participates in this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing ideas and code.
- Focus criticism on behavior and technical outcomes, not people.
- Protect private information and credentials shared by mistake.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's
private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions and participation that violate these
expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method listed in the repository
security policy.
